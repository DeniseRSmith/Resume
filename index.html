The old process was a single unbroken chain — 16 handoffs, ~20 steps counting rework loops, and one blocked step stalled every step behind it. Nobody had ever asked which of those 16 steps actually had to happen in order.

The diagnosis — real dependency vs. inherited habit

I audited all 16 phases and found only 4 true hard gates — Data/Segmentation Requirements, Legal Review, Compliance Review, and Final Approval — that legitimately required specific inputs or sign-off before the next step could begin. The other 12 were running in sequence purely because that's how the process had always been built, not because anything required it. ■ hard gate marks the real constraints below; everything else was free to move.

Left unchallenged, that distinction would never have surfaced — the same ~20-step, ~4-week relay would still be running today, campaign after campaign, simply because "that's the process."
